Why identity and access

Most breaches don't start with a clever exploit, rather, they start with an account that had more access than it should have, or a credential that should've been revoked and wasn't. That's the problem IAM exists to solve, and it's the corner of security I want to spend my career in.

I'm building toward it from the ground up: systems and network fundamentals first, then the identity-specific layer — directory services, provisioning, certificates, and the policies that decide who gets access to what.

  • Studying for CompTIA Security+ as my first industry certification.
  • Practicing Active Directory and Group Policy as a base for identity administration.
  • Building out PKI and certificate labs to understand how trust gets established between systems.
  • Looking for hands-on experience that puts these fundamentals into practice.
